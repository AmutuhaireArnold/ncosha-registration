Start Your Journey Toward a Safer, Healthier Workplace

Welcome — we're glad you're considering the Certificate in Environment, Health and Safety (EHS) with NCOSHA. This registration takes about ten minutes, and it's the first step toward a recognised qualification that employers across Uganda trust.

The EHS programme is a hands-on, competency-based course built around real workplace scenarios. Through expert-led lectures, practical demonstrations, field attachments, and case studies, you'll build the skills to identify hazards, protect worker health, manage environmental risk, and respond confidently when things go wrong. No prior background is required — the programme welcomes learners from every academic and professional path.

  • Morning Programme — 6 weeks
  • Evening Programme — 6 weeks
  • Weekend Programme — 10 weekends

Classes combine face-to-face teaching with practical exercises and a field attachment. To earn your Certificate and Safety Card, you'll need at least 90% attendance and an overall assessment score of 80% or higher.

Fill in the form below with accurate, up-to-date details. Once you submit, our team will follow up within two working days with your intake confirmation, payment steps, and reporting date.
